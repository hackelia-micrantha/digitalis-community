# Community Code of Conduct

Digitalis Community is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and inclusive public project environment.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and assume good faith while evaluating claims critically;
- focus discussion on observable behavior, evidence, risks, and project outcomes;
- welcome questions and differing levels of experience;
- give actionable feedback without personal attacks;
- respect privacy, coordinated disclosure, licensing, and publication boundaries;
- correct mistakes openly and help maintain an accurate public record.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, discrimination, threats, or targeted intimidation;
- insulting or demeaning personal commentary;
- publishing private information without permission;
- deliberately exposing secrets, private implementation material, or unsafe exploit details;
- sustained disruption, spam, impersonation, or bad-faith manipulation of project processes.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **security.digitalis@micrantha.com**. Maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation, close discussions, or restrict access when necessary to protect participants and the project.

Reports will be handled with discretion. Retaliation against a person who raises a good-faith concern is prohibited.
