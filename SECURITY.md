# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not report suspected vulnerabilities in public issues, pull requests, discussions, or social channels.

Email **security.digitalis@micrantha.com** with:

- a clear description of the issue;
- affected public URL, document, release, or component;
- reproduction steps or proof of concept where safe;
- potential impact;
- suggested remediation, if known;
- a preferred contact method.

The canonical machine-readable contact is published at:

`https://digitalis.micrantha.com/.well-known/security.txt`

## Scope

This repository is the public publication and community boundary. Reports may cover:

- the public website and deployment configuration;
- published release artifacts and provenance;
- accidental disclosure of private or sensitive material;
- security guidance that could materially mislead implementers;
- integrity or authenticity problems in public artifacts.

Private implementation vulnerabilities may be routed internally after initial review. Do not include secrets, customer data, raw attestation evidence, or unnecessary exploit detail in the first report.

## Disclosure

Please allow maintainers a reasonable opportunity to investigate and coordinate remediation before public disclosure. Publication timing will account for user risk, available mitigations, and affected downstream consumers.

## Supported versions

Digitalis does not currently publish a production-supported SDK or stable compatibility line. Public artifacts should be treated according to their explicit design, prototype, or preview status.
